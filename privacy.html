Sculpt privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What Sculpt collects

Nothing is sent to us. Your profile, food logs, workouts, measurements and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app’s own storage. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Search online

If you tap Search online, the words you searched for are sent to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to find foods. No other information about you is sent. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Backups

Backups are files you create yourself. When you save one to Google Drive, WhatsApp, email or your Downloads, it goes only where you choose.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Your data → Erase all data, or uninstall the app and clear its storage. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete.

Health

Sculpt gives general guidance, not medical advice. If you’re pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, or have kidney disease, diabetes, a heart condition or a history of eating disorders, check your plan with a doctor first.

Children

Sculpt is meant for adults and is not designed for children under 18.
